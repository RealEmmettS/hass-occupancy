Read the evidence in order

Good input coverage comes before a confident accuracy claim.

  1. Check the observations

    Review selected residents, missing readings, and tracker agreement.

  2. Compare completed predictions

    Use the forecast page’s near-term and hourly accuracy views. Check both error and published coverage against staying put.

  3. Keep the limits in view

    A blank forecast or unknown ETA is not a prediction. Minute sampling can miss short trips or forecast revisions. Cooling energy savings remain untested.
