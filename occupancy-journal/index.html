How to evaluate

Open Forecast & accuracy charts → Was it right? to compare served hourly predictions with later observed occupancy. A blank curve or unknown ETA is not a prediction. Models require at least 10 days of usable history and must pass their publication checks.

The built-in chart keeps 30 days. This journal samples once a minute and preserves changed forecast snapshots (including issue time and ETA), presence and cooling observations for 90 days. It can miss forecast revisions or trips shorter than the sample interval. Exports use UTC timestamps; this page shows your browser's local time.

Review input coverage first, then +1, +3 and +6 hour accuracy and the share of hours that actually receive forecasts. Check arrivals against phone history and compare with a simple “same as now” prediction. Cooling energy savings remain untested.
